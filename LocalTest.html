❓ Help & Guide ›
🚀 Getting Started
Before anything else, go to Settings → Your Info and enter your name and title. This appears on every exported PDF.

Then go to Settings → Houses & Individuals and add each residential site. Under each house, add the individuals who live there. Once that's done, your home screen will show all your houses and the app is ready to use.
🏠 Home Screen
The ring in the top right shows your overall completion percentage for the current month across all houses.

Each house card shows whether the DA3 checklist is done and how many money audits are complete. A red NOs badge means one or more audit items were flagged.

My Tasks shows your personal reminders. Use the filter button to switch between Today & Tomorrow, This Month, All Tasks, or Hidden. Tap a task to edit it.

Calendar shows events synced from your Outlook calendar. Use the button to cycle between Today & Tomorrow, Show All, or Hidden.

The 💾 Save All button at the bottom manually pushes all your data to the cloud immediately, bypassing the usual auto-save delay.
📋 DA3 Monthly Checklist
Tap a house on the home screen, then tap the DA3 Monthly Checklist card. Work through each section — mark items as Done or N/A, and add comments in the text boxes.

When finished, tap Export Filled PDF. On iPhone, the share sheet will pop up automatically — tap Mail or Outlook to email it directly, or tap Save to Files to keep a copy.

You can re-export any past report from History at any time using the download button next to the report.
💰 Money Audits
Tap a house, then tap an individual's name under Money Audits. Enter the cash on hand and Acumatica amounts — the app will flag a mismatch automatically.

Work through items 1–15, marking YES, NO, or N/A. Any NO answer will show a corrective action field — fill this in to document what was done.

When finished, tap Export Filled PDF. The share sheet will pop up — tap Outlook or Mail to send, or Save to Files to keep a copy.

Previously completed audits can be viewed or re-exported from History.
📂 History
Use the arrows at the top to browse previous months. The dashboard shows how many DA3 checklists and money audits were completed for that month.

Any missing reports are listed under the dashboard — these are houses or individuals that have no completed record for that month.

Tap View to see a report, or the ⬇ button to re-export it as a PDF. Use ⬆ Share All to batch export all reports for the current month at once.
📌 My Tasks
Tap + Add Task to create a reminder. Tasks can repeat weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or daily — or be set as a one-time date.

Use the filter button to control what you see: Today & Tomorrow, This Month, All Tasks, or Hidden.

Tap the checkbox to mark a task done for the current cycle. It will reappear on its next scheduled date. Tap the task name to edit or delete it.
📅 Outlook Calendar Sync
To connect your Outlook calendar, follow these steps:

1. Go to outlook.office.com in a browser
2. Click the gear icon (Settings) → View all settings
3. Go to Calendar → Shared calendars
4. Under Publish a calendar, select your calendar and set permissions
5. Click Publish — copy the ICS link that appears
6. Change webcal:// at the start of the link to https://
7. Paste it into Settings → Outlook Calendar → Calendar ICS URL
8. Turn on Auto-sync to refresh automatically each hour

Events will appear in the Calendar section on your home screen. Tap 🔄 Sync Calendar Now in Settings to refresh manually.
📝 Notes
The Notes tab lets you keep quick notes for anything — follow-ups, observations, reminders. You can attach a photo to any note.

Notes can be linked to a specific house or kept general. They are saved with your data on this device, along with everything else.
✍️ Signatures
Go to Settings → Signatures and tap + Draw New Signature. Draw your signature with your finger, then save it.

You can save up to 10 signatures. Each time you export a PDF, one is randomly selected and stamped onto the signature line automatically — you don't need to sign manually.

Tap any saved signature thumbnail to delete it.
📄 Receiving Log & Invoices
The Receiving Log is accessible from the bottom of the home screen. Use it to log incoming invoices and track deliveries for each house.

Photos can be attached to each entry for documentation. Entries are stored with your data on this device.
💾 Data & Backup
Go to Settings → Data Sync / Backup → Export Data Backup to download a copy of all your data as a JSON file. Save this somewhere safe — email it to yourself or put it in Google Drive.

To restore or move to a new device, use Import Data Backup. Choose Full Overwrite to replace everything, or Smart Merge to combine data keeping the most recent version of each record.

All data lives only on this device's local storage — there is no cloud sync, so export a backup regularly and before switching devices.
🌙 Appearance
Go to Settings → Appearance to switch between light and dark mode. The app defaults to dark mode.
